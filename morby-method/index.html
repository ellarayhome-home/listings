Ground rules for everyone

  1. Title goes through the TC. Please do not email title directly about the file. If title reaches out, forward it to the TC.
  2. Cc the TC on any email to the lender or between the team.
  3. Changed by text or call? Email the TC what changed, same day.
  4. Lender change? Tell the TC right away. It changes which documents are prepared.
  5. Two sets of wires. EMD and closing wiring instructions are different. Confirm each by phone with title before sending funds.

Checklists

Find your role. Each list is what you own on the file.

TCBrokerTitle BuyerTransactional Lender

TC

10 items

Coordinates the file from contract to close

  • Open escrow and order the title search
  • Work with title to clear any title issues
  • Follow up with title on the seller's payoffs
  • Request EMD wiring instructions, confirm the EMD was sent, and send the receipt to everyone
  • Collect the buyer's corporate docs and send them to title and the broker
  • Write up the seller finance contracts, get them notarized, and record them
  • Collect signing times and follow up with title on the notaries
  • Review and balance the settlement statement, and confirm the surplus with the buyer
  • Send the wiring forms and build the payout sheet with everyone's wiring instructions
  • Request funding from the transactional lender and keep everyone updated

Broker

6 items
  • Order the insurance, survey, and appraisal
  • Serve as the main contact with the lender and provide what the lender needs
  • If the appraisal comes in low, work with the lender to raise it
  • Pass the corporate docs from the TC along to the lender
  • Let the TC know if the lender requires in-person signing and whether they will disburse on scanbacks
  • Email the TC any update that happens by text or call

Title

7 items
  • Open escrow and run the title search
  • Work with the TC to clear any title issues
  • Order payoffs for the seller's open mortgages and liens
  • Provide the EMD wiring instructions and the EMD receipt
  • Provide separate closing wiring instructions
  • Set up the seller and buyer notaries
  • Close the file and disburse funds per the settlement statement and payout sheet

Buyer

8 items
  • Send the TC the buying entity's corporate docs
  • Tell the TC who the transactional lender is, at the start and again at clear to close
  • Wire the EMD
  • Fill out and return the wiring form
  • Give the TC signing times
  • Confirm the surplus with the TC
  • Pay the third-party fees for the seller finance docs (attorney, notary, recording)
  • Email the TC any update that happens by text or call

Transactional Lender

5 items
  • Let the TC know the lender's requirements up front, including whether the seller carry docs must be signed first, so the proper process is followed
  • Fill out and return the wiring form
  • Confirm the wire amount
  • Confirm the return
  • Fund when title gives the all clear
